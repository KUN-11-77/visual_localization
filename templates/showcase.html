Integrated Methods

Methods newly integrated into the XRLocalization pipeline (not present in upstream HLoc or XRLocalization).

EigenPlaces Retrieval

Unsupervised VPR using ResNet-50 + GeM pooling. Trained on GSV-Cities. More viewpoint-invariant than NetVLAD for cross-scene generalization.

CricaVPR Retrieval

Cross-image relational contrastive VPR using DINOv2 ViT-B backbone. Leverages cross-batch relationships for more discriminative global descriptors.

ALIKED Detector

A Lighter and Improved Keypoint Extraction with Descriptors. Neural keypoint detector with deformable convolution, faster than SuperPoint while maintaining comparable repeatability.

LightGlue Matcher

Lightweight transformer-based feature matcher. Adaptive depth and width based on input difficulty — faster than SuperGlue on easy pairs while matching its accuracy on hard pairs.
